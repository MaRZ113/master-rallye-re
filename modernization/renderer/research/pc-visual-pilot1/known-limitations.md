# Exact limits

* Target native draw identity and course/resource owner are unproved. No activated material feature is shipped.
* Source signatures are float32 bit-exact PC coordinates, not the tolerance-based PS2 matching key. Transformed/repacked geometry may need independent matrix/upload correlation.
* 128 candidate probes/frame,256 faces/call,2048 vertices/call,1MiB copied bytes/frame. A missing late target can be a budget limit, not absence.
* WRITEONLY,dynamic,GPU-only,unknown-FVF,UP/strips/large groups cannot supply content in this first probe. State/range-only evidence remains insufficient. No alternate lock flags/readback are attempted.
* Creation serials do not track every update or raw resource Release/COM escape. No persistent activation token is issued.
* Original PC winding duplication, native culling, source texture/vertex alpha, transparent queue order and overlapping foliage remain distinct issues.
* Readable-resource locks/getters may add bounded latency during opt-in F10. This is diagnostic-only, not a proposed per-frame interception predicate.
* Live PC/PCSX2 validation, exact GS parity and visual acceptance are pending. Synthetic native captures are not game evidence.
