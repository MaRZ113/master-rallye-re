# Texture policy

Stock PC texture remains bound by the game. There is no texture replacement, GXI upload, mip change or alpha remapping. Optional Mode2 is deferred and rejected to Stock.

TREEBLEND1 source facts:PS2 France1 bush01 GXI64×64,89 stored alpha levels versus PC DXT128×128,15 levels. Image equivalence is not established. Neither stored alpha nor conventional D3D8 blend selectors establishes exact PS2 sampled alpha. Original files/pixels are excluded from Git and the handoff. The probe records bound texture pointer and observed creation generation, not an invented resource filename.
