All 33 paired GXI/DXT nonmatches in 8.4.1 are fonts. Some have dimensions that differ; their DXT CRC still matches its own payload. This is evidence of an out-of-sync or otherwise distinct compiled counterpart, not evidence that the GXI parser is wrong. One unpaired GXI in `demo-8.4.1:DataGx/Test/Jump.oldf/` is structurally truncated relative to its declared dimensions.

`demo-8.4.1:DataGx/Vehicles/Trooper/complete.gxm` and `demo-8.4.1:DataGx/Vehicles/Trooper/TrooperFrontEnd.gxm` are byte-identical, both 260,215 bytes, SHA256 `002c3d8affafbb4691ce3487f9947f162eff0e6d9f5e0f192316e794d4e9cac3`. This is a **CONFIRMED_BY_BYTES** alias for that build only.

The direct-stem pair evidence is in `resource-pairs.json`; no absent target is filled from another corpus. GXP uses a separate indexed-tile pattern: 31 November GXP files reproduce 146 same-build DXT tiles byte-identically; see `gxp-dxt-tiles.md` and `gxp-dxt-tiles.json`.
