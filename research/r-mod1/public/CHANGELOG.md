# Changelog

## v0.1.0-beta.1

- First public beta of the standalone Randomizer & AI Extender.
- Adds per-mode Stock, T1, T2, T3, Full, Mixed, and Diverse AI policies.
- Adds a Quick Race opponent menu ceiling of up to five AI by default.
- Preserves native event rosters in later Cup and Master Rallye stages.
- Keeps Challenge Preview and its race opponent synchronized.
- Supports live Randomizer policy changes at new roster boundaries.
- Uses the original retail executable without changing it on disk.
- Coexists with the existing graphical `d3d8.dll` proxy.

Known limitation: seven-AI races have shown instability with some T1/T2
player-car setups. The exact cause is unknown.
