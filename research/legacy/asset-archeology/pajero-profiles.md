# Pajero legacy profile archaeology

Legacy beta-profile v9 describes inferred Pajero body branches
`licensed_red`, `fictional_blue`, `final_yellow`, and
`preview_hybrid`, plus wheel profiles `current_txt`,
`promo_pswheel64`, `promo_pswheel64_fit`, and
`alt_pswheel64_2`.

These names are **LEGACY_HEURISTIC** labels. They are not format fields and do
not prove release chronology.

The read-only modern audit found 1,143 vehicle DXT resources overall. Pajero
contains two resources unreferenced by any currently parsed DX or TXT tuple:
`bonblue-tga.dxt` and `pathe-tga.dxt`. The name-only alternative detector
flags `bonblue-tga.dxt`; `pathe-tga.dxt` remains merely apparently
unreferenced. Other blue/yellow/wheel resources used by the legacy profiles are
referenced by at least one existing TXT or DX source and therefore are not
classified as unused.

Confidence: **MEDIUM** that the files represent alternate art assets;
**UNKNOWN** for their exact beta/final chronology and intended runtime
selection. No texture or screenshot is stored in Git.
