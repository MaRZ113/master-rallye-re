# Draw serialization: rev131 versus rev135

Evidence is limited to same-source Trooper `car`, `complete`, and `wheel`
outputs. Their GXM source hashes are equal across the supplied generation
folders; pair provenance and hashes are recorded in `input-manifest.json`.

## Mapped record boundaries

| Resource | Mapped flat tag-2 draws | Per-record growth | Legacy prefix bytes | Rev135 parsed prefix bytes |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| car | 22 | +13 | 11 | 24 |
| complete | 20 | +13 | 11 | 24 |
| wheel | 5 | +13 | 11 | 24 |

Each mapped record has the same first 20 bytes in both versions. In the
existing reader these five 32-bit values are tag, vertex base, local vertex
maximum, index start, and index count. Texture-reference suffixes beginning
with the first length-prefixed name are byte-identical for all 47 records.
The record sequence and core-to-draw association are stable in these samples.

## Prefix bytes

The rev131 bytes after the shared 20-byte core and before the first texture
string are retained as opaque. Observed values (hex, with record counts):

| Resource | Rev131 opaque prefix variants |
|---|---|
| car | `0001010700000003000000` × 15; `0001010300000003000000` × 6; `0101010700000003000000` × 1 |
| complete | `0001010700000003000000` × 17; `0001010300000003000000` × 2; `0101010700000003000000` × 1 |
| wheel | `0000010500000003000000` × 5 |

Rev135 exposes a 24-byte pre-string prefix at draw-relative offsets `0x14`
through `0x2B`. The parser currently reads it as six four-byte fields:

| Draw-relative offset | Parser field | Observed values |
|---:|---|---|
| `0x14` | `unknown_0x14` | 1 |
| `0x18` | `unknown_0x18` | 0 |
| `0x1C` | `unknown_0x1c_float` | 1.0 |
| `0x20` | `flags_0x20` | resource-specific raw bytes; retained as hex |
| `0x24` | `unknown_0x24` | 3, 5, or 7 |
| `0x28` | texture slot count | 3 |

The exact rev135 bytes are recorded per draw in `dx-binary-diff.json`. The
observed record model is therefore:

```text
rev131: 20-byte shared core + 11 opaque bytes + identical texture suffix
rev135: 20-byte shared core + 24-byte parsed prefix + identical texture suffix
```

This accounts for the 13-byte growth but does not prove the old-to-new field
mapping or whether those values are copied, initialized, or recomputed.

## Index and topology relation

All positions, normals, colors, and UVs are byte-identical in source vertex
order. The local uint16 index values differ, but corresponding draws have the
same oriented triangle multiset with winding preserved. Counts are 1,911
triangles (car), 2,375 (complete), and 252 (wheel). The JSON maps each old
triangle ordinal to its matching new ordinal. Thus this sample is an in-draw
triangle-order change, not a demonstrated topology change.

The trailing uint32 index block is byte-identical after relocation by the
draw-section growth. The current DX parser's local/global reconstruction
check reports mismatches for every changed local index position. Its expected
relationship is therefore not promoted as a valid rev135 invariant.

## Evidence limits

- Flat tag-2 records are aligned by equal count, record-size growth, shared
  core, parsed rev135 record boundaries, and identical texture suffix.
- No draw-record ordering change was observed in these three resources.
- `unknown_*` and raw flag fields remain unresolved.
- The comparison does not establish what a retail reader requires or why it
  rejects raw rev131 output.
- Do not generalize these values to all resources or future revisions without
  another controlled source/output pair.
