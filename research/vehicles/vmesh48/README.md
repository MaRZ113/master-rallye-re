# V-MESH4.8 — mask coverage, lamp continuity, stock tyres and decal restoration

User runtime feedback on V4.7 confirms removal of nonreflective body patches. Remaining observations: see-through front/hood/plate gaps, triangular lamp joins, uneven decal sharpness, missing rear graphic, filled gold spoke gaps, and unsuitable procedural tyre imagery. Wheel size is accepted. This phase preserves the accepted size and the window closure policy.

## Hood correction after experimental V48 feedback

The black U-shaped hood patch in v48-exp-002 was generated backing visible through a wrongly cut-out painted region. Its source clear pixels all carry blue RGB(0,23,65), close to the dominant opaque paint RGB(0,27,69). An opt-in `solid_paint_control_alpha` heuristic restores coverage only for exclusive paint materials with a mixed alpha channel, one chromatic clear colour, dominant opaque colour support of at least50%, and RGB distance at most8. Binary decals, glass/shared-role materials, black voids and dissimilar cavity colours stay excluded. This is a palette inference, not recovered GT3 runtime semantics. Only one current source material qualifies;4490 clear texels become solid. Source RGB, vertices and UVs remain unchanged. No generated black upper-paint underlays remain in the final candidate. Air intake geometry is retained.

## Reusable corrections

- Optional `reflection_in_helpers` separates structural mask coverage from reflectance. Paint texture alpha becomes255/0; reflection strength0.58 moves to normalized helper RGB. With the native GREATER128 test, alpha148 required about86% filtered coverage to survive; alpha255 requires50%. Retained helper consumers are reported, and tail-lamp alpha is compensated where it shares a scaled helper. This fixes the verified coupled-control policy; game/mipmap behavior remains a human-test boundary.
- Bright binary-alpha paint decal materials get priority in the largest existing non-environment atlas. Structural paint masks keep reflective capacity. Existing small badges/STI keep their previous texel footprint. The finite atlas budget moves24 interior-mask triangles into a32-square blend atlas, lowering their detail; this is a documented tradeoff. All fixed donor texture sizes, ordered bindings and native draw identities stay preserved. Available source detail and finite atlas capacity still limit maximum sharpness.
- Lamp glass now welds coincident source positions and averages their normals before its1mm offset. A common edge cannot receive different per-triangle offsets. The glass material remains native alpha blend; opaque housing remains inside.
- Bounded collars around dark planar terminal recess panels provide interior closure; the experimental black upper-paint underlays were removed from the final candidate. Collars are derived per connected source component rather than relying only on near-parallel panel edges. Existing source vertices/UVs are not moved. Generated geometry has separate provenance.
- Tyre tread returns byte-for-byte from the Master Rallye donor. The donor sidewall annulus is sampled into the accepted rim profile; its original silver disc is excluded. The GT3 disc's original alpha mask selects dark opaque RGB between spokes, with zero reflection there. Wheel alpha blend/test gates remain off; this is not a transparent opening.
- `vmesh_decal_authoring.py` supports explicit private source-artwork composition and geometry-based binding to a clear central rear placeholder. The current dump has a1x1 clear placeholder instead of the rear graphic. A private authored composition of the existing livery crescent/tails, stars and SUBARU lettering supplies the missing decal. It is an approximation from source artwork, not a recovered original rear texture. Inputs/profile/pixels remain ignored.
