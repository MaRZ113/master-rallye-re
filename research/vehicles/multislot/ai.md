# R5V-I.0 AI roster composition

The candidate appends physical ID27 only to the T2 arms of the existing
dynamic selectors: Quick Race, a newly generated Rallye Cup roster, and a
newly created Master Rallye competition. It routes ID27 through the existing
native exclusion/append body, retaining duplicate/human exclusions, driver
selection and roster publication. It does not force an AI slot and does not
change an already stored Cup/Master roster or stage lifecycle.

Normal Invitation remains T3-only and is not changed. Challenge stays
authored. T2 eligibility is not gated on the player having selected ID27;
class reachability and the ID10 unlock policy remain distinct. These are
static candidate properties, not runtime findings. The I.0 proof may use the
vehicle as a selected player car; natural AI inclusion can be separately
observed if it occurs without forcing a participant.
