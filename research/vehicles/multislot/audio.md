# R5V-I.0 audio composition

ID27's provisional audio profile is stock tuned profile7 because the I.0
diagnostic record currently uses Navara physical ID7 as its donor. The
candidate preserves physical IDs and maps only the audio-profile selector;
ID26 remains on its established stock profile0. No audio resources or audio
selection policy are modified.

This is not a sound qualification for a real ID27 vehicle. A distinct T2
vehicle must receive an independently selected or configured audio family
under the existing G.2 architecture before the generic addon SDK can close.
