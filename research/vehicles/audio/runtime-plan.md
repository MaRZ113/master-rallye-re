## Pass criteria

* Both candidates keep `Race/Car0/CarID=26`, class 0, and Mercedes runtime
  model/wheel/physics identity.
* The ID26 untuned warning disappears through the real tuned code path.
* Candidate A is audibly ordinary; Candidate B is audibly distinct and
  preferably bass-heavy.
* No crash, silence, stuck revs or obvious per-frame instability occurs during
  the short drive.
