# R-COOKER2 — DX revision 131 to 135 upgrader

**Status: `PRODUCTION_READY_FOR_SUPPORTED_VEHICLE_DX` (scope-limited).**
The converter passed the currently available supported vehicle corpus and the
minimal transition has runtime evidence for two families. It is not a general
converter for all Master Rallye DX files, and it does not reproduce the
official 9.10.0 triangle optimizer.

## R-COOKER1.2 closeout

Forester `car.dx`, `complete.dx`, and `wheel.dx` candidates generated by the
unchanged R-COOKER1.1 prototype passed retail runtime testing. The frontend
complete, race model, wheel model, textures/material appearance, and reported
geometry worked with no visible model problems. Wheel placement, collision,
damage, and crash behavior were not separately reported. Together with the
Trooper runtime result, the no-reorder conversion is `CONFIRMED_BY_RUNTIME`
for the exact tested Trooper and Forester candidate bytes. R-COOKER1.2 is
closed; neither result implies universal DX support.

## Supported transformation

For the supported flat revision-131 vehicle grammar, the converter:

1. changes header revision `131` to `135`;
2. expands every 11-byte prefix `A B C | u32 X | u32 texture_slot_count`
   into `u32 1 | u32 0 | float32 1.0 | A 00 B C | u32 X | u32 slot_count`;
3. preserves draw cores and length-prefixed texture references;
4. preserves geometry, vertex attributes, local uint16 index order, collision,
   global-index table, and trailing bytes.

The source parser `inspect_demo_dx` establishes the source-era section
boundaries. The converter validates the observed flat tag-2 draw records and
their index/vertex ranges, checks all records consume the complete draw
region, and then validates the result with `parse_dx_bytes`. Canonical or
collision warnings/errors, inconsistent global indices, unknown record tags,
invalid ranges, unsupported revisions, and malformed prefixes fail closed.
It does not use official rev135 bytes to build output.

Every conversion report records hashes, sizes, revisions, draw count,
canonical parser status, collision tags, and explicit preservation checks.
Single-file writes are atomic and refuse existing paths unless `--force` is
given. In-place conversion is always refused. Directory output is staged and
renamed only after all discovered DX roles validate. Directory mode only
handles direct `car.dx`, `complete.dx`, and `wheel.dx` files; other resources,
including DXT, are neither copied nor changed. A validated rev135 role is
copied byte-for-byte in directory mode; single-file mode rejects it as
already converted.

## Corpus and pair evidence

The current corpus scan covered 38 rev131 vehicle DX files: 21 from `inputs/`
and 17 from the read-only Demo 9.3.1 `DataGx/Vehicles` tree. Seven source
families are represented. All 38 pass the promoted converter and canonical
rev135 parser without diagnostics; no unknown vehicle draw variant was
observed. The parser does not expose a separate material-record count.

Among paired outputs in `inputs/`, 10 roles have byte-identical GXM source and
were compared. Two `complete.gxm` pairs differ in source hash and were blocked
from same-source conclusions. All 135 paired draw records match the prefix
formula. Six of the 10 same-GXM pairs also have identical GXI sidecar
inventories; sidecar differences in the remaining pairs are kept separate
from the GXM identity result. In all eligible comparisons, geometry attribute
arrays and collision/tail/global sections are accounted for; candidate vs
official rev135 byte changes are confined to local uint16 index ordering.
The JSON report preserves per-file hashes and exact pair findings.

## Runtime boundary

Trooper and Forester package outputs staged by R-COOKER2 have hashes matching
their earlier runtime-tested candidates. The other five staged family
packages are static candidates only. `NOT_ASSESSED_BY_CONVERTER` in a generic
conversion manifest means that the converter itself did not perform or
infer a runtime test.

## Readiness decision

**A — `PRODUCTION_READY_FOR_SUPPORTED_VEHICLE_DX`.** This decision applies to
the observed flat tag-2 vehicle revision-131 grammar represented by the 38
scanned files. It does not extend to non-vehicle revision-131 files, unknown
future layouts, every retail asset pairing, or every executable build. The
exact official triangle reorder remains intentionally omitted. The two
different-source `complete.gxm` pairs remain excluded from same-source
comparison; they do not constitute a rejected rev131 grammar variant.

No production upgrader integration into Vehicle Composer, release, or cooker
executable analysis is part of this phase. The operator may choose optional
runtime testing of the additional static family candidates as a later review
step.
