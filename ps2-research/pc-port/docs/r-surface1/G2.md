# R-SURFACE1 G2 — dynamic grime

Status: **G2.2 BUILT (DLL 391a7e47), awaiting the owner's race review** (2026-11-12). The owner accepted G1.5 on the retail cars. Light dust on the 4Runner and the
Evo IV remains, which the owner attributes to their materials (they barely reflect).

## What changes

Every race car, AI included, now has its own grime state. In G1 every car had the same fixed `StartLevel`.

* **State**: `grime::CarGrime` (`renderer/vehicle_grime.hpp`). It holds 8 zones (front low, front arch, side low left/right, rear arch, rear,
  bonnet, roof), each with dust, mud, wet and mud age. G0 already computed this state, but only as a dry run.
* **Signals**: the particle triangles that the game draws near each car, with the G0 identification by level-0 texture SHA and attribution to the nearest Broker car
  position within `ProbeRadiusCm`. Every `SampleMs` (500 ms) window, `grime::accumulate()` applies them:
  * dust, mud, stones and grass build grime up (lower body and arches first, roof last);
  * water splashes add wet and wash mud and dust off;
  * wet decays over `DryTime` (default 45 s);
  * mud age grows, so the tint of dried mud gets lighter over about 90 s.
  * Paused and replay time never counts.
* **The simulation runs without the G0 log**: it is active with `Probe=1`, or with `Enabled=1` and `Dynamic=1`. The `grime-*.jsonl` diagnostics are written only with `Probe=1`.
* **Start**: Quick Race and attract start from `RaceStart` (0 = clean). In the Master Rallye mode the saved checkpoint is restored. That G0 rule
  (campaign + checkpoint key, `MRRRenderer\grime\master-rallye-grime.txt`) now drives the picture, so a stage left and loaded again shows the same
  dirt.
* **Drawn car → race car**: `car_index()` picks the Broker car whose physics position is nearest to the drawn WORLD translation, within 6 m. The answer is cached per
  instance and race and re-checked every draw. An unmatched instance gets no grime.

## Pass (same post-draw pass as G1)

| Stage | Colour | Alpha |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | DIFFUSE × TFACTOR (car tint) | TFACTOR.a = kind factor (× texture alpha for Grille/Decal) |
| 1 | current | × car zone texture (64×32, clamp): the per-zone threshold level |
| 2 | MODULATE2X noise detail | ADDSIGNED2X(noise atlas, current) = 2 (noise + level − .5) |

* Zone texture texel: `zone_weight(u, v)` / .9 × `zone_amount(u, v)`.
  * u runs from the rear (0) to the front (1, +z); v runs from the roof down.
  * The zone amount is dust + mud of the zone, interpolated over the body: rear, rear arch, sides, front arch, front along the lower body; roof to bonnet along the top.
  * Each zone therefore grows its own patches.
* Rebuild: one managed texture per race car (and one for the static look). It is rebuilt only when the state changed, at most once per sample window.
* Tint, `car_tint()`:
  * dust → mud by the mud share;
  * dried mud moves 45 % toward the dust tint over 90 s;
  * wet darkens by up to 35 %;
  * wheels keep the darker tone of G1.
* `Dynamic=0`: the G1 behaviour with the new stage layout (a uniform `StartLevel` texture and `StartMud` tint).

## Config (`VehicleGrime.ini`)

```ini
Dynamic=1      ; 0 = fixed G1 look
RaceStart=0.0  ; Quick Race / attract start (0 = clean)
Rate=1.0       ; accumulation multiplier
DryTime=45     ; seconds
```

## Not yet

* Per-zone tint (the tint is per car).
* Left/right side difference (the side zones are averaged).
* Wet sheen and the wiper arc (G3).
* PS2 fleet bodies (private draws).
* The Service Park wash trigger (G4; `CarGrime::wash()` is ready).

The rates are the G0 calibration: a dense dust trail reaches about 85 % in a minute. They are to be tuned on owner runs with `Rate`.

Tests (`tests/ps2_diag_tests.cpp`): zone interpolation (rear only, sides low, roof clean), zone texels, tint (dust, mud, dried lighter, wet darker),
TFACTOR packing, start state.

## G2.1 — owner review (2026-11-13)

The owner reported that G2 looks good. Requests:
* tyres got grime unevenly;
* the grime tone should depend on the surface;
* grime should land on the body differently;
* PS2 fleet bodies should get grime;
* PS2 reflections disappear under dust.

* **Tyres**: the Wheel kind samples one atlas point (`uniform_atlas_uv()`: coverage noise about .25 of .5, neutral detail), so the whole tread gets an
  even layer. The car's lower-body level sets its density. Rims keep the patchy look.
* **Surface tone**:
  * Every particle triangle carries a colour: the alpha-weighted mean of its texture × its vertex colour (courses tint their particles).
  * `accumulate()` blends that colour into the car's `dust_rgb` (dust, stones) and `mud_rgb` (mud, grass, stones) by the share of grime the window added.
  * `surface_tone()` takes the hue from that colour and the brightness from the configured tint. `[Look] SurfaceTint` (default .75) is the share; 0 = RGB values only.
  * The colours are not persisted.
* **Directional build-up**: the probe records whether each particle was ahead of or behind the car and on its left or right (MR is left-handed, y up:
  right of heading (hx, hz) is (hz, −hx)). `direction_weights()` scales the zones by .5..1.5:
  * front low, front arch and bonnet by the front share;
  * rear arch and rear by the rear share;
  * the side zones by their side's share.

  The own trail dirties the rear, another car's trail the front.
* **Left/right on the body**:
  * With `D3DPTEXTURECAPS_VOLUMEMAP`, the zone texture is a 64×32×2 volume: slice 0 = left side zone, slice 1 = right, filtered across the width
    (`ramp_matrix3`, w = car-local x across the bounds, stage 1 `COUNT3`).
  * Without volume support it stays 2D with the sides averaged.
  * `grime_zone_texture` logs the choice.
* **PS2 fleet bodies**: `draw_car_fleet` (`race_car_fleet_native.hpp`) calls `grime_fleet()` after each body mesh it submits, layer 0 only.
  * `atmospherebody::fleet_vertices()` now returns the registry capability it used.
  * Role → kind: paint, panel, trim, flat, cover → Body; rubber → Wheel; metal → Rim. Crew and interior are excluded by texture name.
  * The pass is the same `grime_pass()` with `DrawPrimitiveUP` on the same vertex array.
  * Bounds come from the body meshes; the car is matched to the race car by position like the PC cars.
  * PS2 lamps, glass and PS2 wheels are not covered yet.
* **PS2 reflections under dust**: the PS2 reflection is applied and removed inside the native draw, before the grime pass, so its states are untouched.
  It disappeared because a full dust patch was opaque and covered the paint together with its reflection.
  * A fourth stage now multiplies the coverage by `TFACTOR.a` = kind factor × car opacity.
  * Opacity is `[Look] DustOpacity` (default .8) for pure dust and 1 for pure mud, so a dusty car keeps a dimmed reflection.
  * The kind factors (glass .6, lamp .7, rim .4, ...) are now opacities. In G1 they lowered the patch threshold.
  * The pass needs 4 blend stages (`grime_overlay_caps`).
* Code: the pass is `grime_pass(d, candidate, draw)` in `atmosphere_native.hpp`. `grime_overlay()` calls it for native draws (with the shell subset) and `grime_fleet()` for UP draws.
* Log: `grime_overlay` adds `fleet` and `volume`.
* Tests:
  * side and direction counts, direction weights and the deposit colour;
  * surface tone (brightness kept, hue taken), opacity, the tyre atlas point, the volume matrix.

## G2.2 — owner review (2026-11-13)

The owner confirmed that grime is visible and the tyres are now even. Requests:
* grass should leave a slightly green, noticeable layer;
* the Astero bull bar and similar parts of other cars got little grime;
* reflections were lost on Kamaz and Astero in one race.

* **Grass**:
  * `Rates::grass` is .00025 (was .00008).
  * Grass deposits `[Look] GrassRGB` (default .34,.42,.18) into the car's mud colour instead of the brown `grassdirt` particle colour, weighted ×3 like its rate.
  * The configured mud brightness is kept (`surface_tone`).
* **Bull bars, bumpers, racks**: chrome/metal (`WheelMetal` role) in the car resource is now Body grime in all three routes (body route, fallback, PS2 fleet). Before, it got the faint
  rim layer (.4) with wheel bounds. Only the wheel resource has the Rim kind.
* **Reflections on Kamaz and Astero (race 3 of the owner's run)**: in that race both cars were PC models with the PS2 reflection applied (ledger
  `reflection_body.applied` 59 937, no failures). The fleet path was not active, and no restore or disable record exists. The cause is not
  established from the log. Changes that remove possible interference:
  * the pass writes colour only (`D3DRS_COLORWRITEENABLE` RGB), so framebuffer alpha is never touched;
  * on PS2 fleet bodies the pass runs after the mesh's last layer, so the weighted env layer (modes 5/21) is under the grime, as on PC.
  * An owner A/B (`Enabled=0`) is requested.
* Test: grass deposits a green mud colour from brown particles.
