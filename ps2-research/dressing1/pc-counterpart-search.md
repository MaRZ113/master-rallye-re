# PC counterpart search

The search examines7,595 retail paths,160 compiled DX resources and270 TXT/XML
files. It hashes46 noncourse/nonvehicle/non-autoshow DX files, covering the
available Misc/shared/frontend model inventory. Thirty-eight text files have
relevant family leads. Exact paths, hashes and searched classes are in
[pc-search-evidence.json](pc-search-evidence.json).

The geometry search decodes complete paired Turkey3/ItalyS1/France1 landscapes
through the read-only SDK. It does not decode every standalone model or all36
landscapes. Filename/material/texture hits are discovery leads. Every negative
statement is limited to these scanned portions.

All four original groups still have zero exact world-position triangle matches.
Hut subparts and the whole boat positive all-corner fits show why that test alone cannot establish
family absence. Exact PC placement, material candidates, shape bounds and
fitted residuals are retained separately from reusable geometry identity.

No matching noncourse PSM family name was found for Turkey hut/boat/shrub or
Italy pinus in the bounded PS2 name inventory. Named standalone dinghies are a
different resource family; Turkey material boat1 is not France Egg boat1.
PC standalone resource existence does not prove a course references or draws it.
All standalone files are inventoried, but universal geometry absence is UNKNOWN.
