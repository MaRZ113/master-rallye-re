# Future content requirements

Hut and boat subparts provide REUSE_EXISTING_PC_MESH candidates. A future content
pack would still need explicit instance/placement selection, material mapping
and safe granularity before reuse or additional authored surfaces. Present
congruency results do not authorize moving current PC scenery or importing PS2
positions. Whole-object correspondence remains NOT_READY.

Shrub/treeblend and pinus/tree source groups require REQUIRES_DEEPER_PS2_RE.
The actual foliage vertex interpretation, camera-dependent representation and
material semantics must be recovered before a source component can be called a
placed plant. A flat D3D8 draw alone is not a reliable material/instance oracle.

France dinghies are conditional REPLACE_BAKED_STATIC_OBJECT and
PC_RUNTIME_CONTROLLER candidates. The missing interface is a verified mapping
from an original source object/route pose to PC compiled subparts. Hiding a whole
shared draw is unsafe for this purpose. Collision is a separate research layer;
no visual-geometry result establishes collision equivalence or creates physics.

Course SDK could later expose source material/group/instance metadata, but its
TXT spans currently are not direct compiled draw identifiers. No SDK extension,
course writer, renderer hook or playable model conversion is performed here.
Every backlog entry is a planning classification and NOT_READY for implementation.
