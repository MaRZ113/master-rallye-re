# Source ownership versus instances

Each mandatory candidate has one material mesh record, many source strips and
an enclosing landscape carrier. The candidate tag2 and its tag5 parent have no
separate authored matrix. All selected course landscapes have independently
validated authored identity matrices and matching source-coordinate anchors
from GEOM1. No additional transform is fitted for world-position comparison.

The113-face Turkey boat source group has one common rigid fit to a PC draw825
subset. Its internal disconnected pieces retain the same relative geometry;
this is stronger than family/subpart similarity. Original instance labels,
placement-authoring ownership and live draw selection are still not recovered.

The proper rigid fits in hut/boat shape diagnostics are separate analytical
relationships. They show that a source subpart is reusable at another location;
they do not recover an original game instance matrix. Every fitted result is
STATIC_INFERENCE, with full corner checks and explicit residuals.

world=local.x*Row0+local.y*Row1+local.z*Row2+Row3. The authored Row3 is overwritten
by the route; it is not a placement oracle. That pose can be nonorthogonal under
the original forward/up rules. A unit rigid shape test is consequently a bounded
geometry test, not a complete rejection of every possible runtime shape relation.
