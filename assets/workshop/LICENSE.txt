Rally Workshop icon artwork is original geometric vector artwork created for
this project in 2026. It contains no game renders, extracted sprites, textures,
models, sponsor logos or manufacturer marks. The broad silhouette evokes an
off-road rally prototype; it does not reproduce a proprietary vehicle asset.

To the extent possible under law, the authors dedicate this icon artwork and
its generated PNG/ICO representations to the public domain under CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The generator tools/generate_workshop_icon.py records all original shape data.
